OKX Unified Tokenized Stocks (currently supported by xStocks / Backed Assets) provide price exposure, not direct ownership, and voting rights are not included unless the issuer expressly says so. When the cash market is closed, liquidity may thin, spreads may widen, and the tokenized price may differ from the latest exchange trade. The product is offered only where it is available and you qualify; it is not available to U.S. persons or in restricted jurisdictions. “Same day stock trading” can describe a venue permission. It should not be written as a change to market settlement rules.

An exemption has to come from the rule it claims to modify

T+0, instant settlement, and same-day trading are search phrases that people often want to hear as product promises. A more careful sentence is: cash-equity accounts follow the settlement and trading arrangements of their market, which can be amended and must be read from official sources at the time; a tokenized venue follows a different matching and account rule set. Even if you can place a second order the same calendar day, that fact should be written as an account state, not as “this product has received a T+0 exemption” or as “settlement is now instant.” Borrowing those names as headlines lends the product a legal status it is not claiming in this guide.

Eligibility and regional limits do not disappear because the search included T+0. You have to be able to make the representations in the terms, and review may still be required. Official materials note that order-book pairs are commonly quoted in USDT. Binance bStocks is a different product and should not be described as a settlement exemption either. If the same-day orders happen outside regular U.S. cash-market hours — NYSE materials describe 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern, subject to that day’s calendar — spread and gap risk should be recorded as a third column, not ignored because the day has not ended. Around-the-clock matching is not, by itself, traditional T+0.

Translate the search into checks, not into a slogan

  • Confirm eligibility first.
  • Keep “does the account allow another ticket today?” and “what does the cash settlement cycle require?” on two lines.
  • If both tickets are off hours, add spread and gap notes.
  • Do not use un-qualified T+0 or instant-settlement promises in descriptions of OKX or of Binance bStocks.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it a problem to say tokenized stocks are T+0?

Because that sentence lends a cash-market rule name to a different product file. Matching and settlement are not the same event.

If I sold on the same day, may I tell others I am doing T+0?

A more accurate sentence is that the venue may have allowed another order that day.

Does this exempt me from traditional settlement timing?

That promise is not made here.

Can Binance bStocks be described with those search phrases as promises?

No. Read Binance’s documents, and avoid un-qualified institutional names.
